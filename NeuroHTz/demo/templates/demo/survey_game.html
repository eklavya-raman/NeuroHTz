Welcome to the Cognitive Assessment

This questionnaire evaluates cognitive response across two parts, each with 3 levels of increasing complexity.

  • Part 1 — Conducted in silence (no auditory stimulation).
  • Stimulation — 15-minute 40 Hz auditory stimulus that cannot be paused or rewound.
  • Part 2 — Conducted after stimulation is complete, in silence (no auditory stimulation).
  • Each part is timed — answer as quickly and accurately as you can.
  • Some levels include memory tasks and listening exercises.
  • Please use headphones during the stimulation phase.
